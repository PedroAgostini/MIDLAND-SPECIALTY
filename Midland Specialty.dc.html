Who We Are

Midland Specialty Inc.

Midland Specialty Inc. is a Hispanic-owned, comprehensive supply chain entity designed to provide our clients with a single trusted source for 3PL logistics solutions. Our senior leadership is led by a retired federal law enforcement professional with deep experience in both domestic and international transportation, bringing a rare understanding of international regulations and foreign government oversight to every shipment we manage. Headquartered in Miami, FL, Midland Specialty provides logistics and supply chain services at any scale, anywhere in the world.

We take pride in partnering directly with our clients, working side by side to solve complex business challenges and deliver a long-term, cost-effective, and efficient custom 3PL solution built around their specific needs. Our commitment is a total turnkey solution that benefits clients through cost savings, reduced time to market, single-supplier management, and a trusted Tier 1 supplier diversity partner relationship.

Leadership

DH

Daktor Holguin

Owner

Daktor Holguin is the Owner of Midland Specialty Inc., built on a vision to give clients real solutions for the challenges that arise as their business grows, from moving and storing cargo to navigating the unexpected world of security and compliance.

Before founding Midland Specialty, Daktor spent 26 years with the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) as a Special Agent, retiring as an Assistant Regional Director. Across those 26 years, his work ethic, relationship-building, and leadership produced an exemplary investigative record. He brings that same experience, network, and leadership to every client relationship at Midland.

MH

Margaret A. Harchut

Chief Administrative Officer

Margaret A. Harchut joined Midland Specialty Inc. to ensure operations run smoothly behind the scenes. She brings 34 years of federal service spanning law enforcement and administrative legal management, with deep experience in complex research, case management, and courtroom coordination.

Margaret applies that same precision and reliability to managing Midland Specialty, ensuring every client receives a seamless experience from first contact through final delivery.
