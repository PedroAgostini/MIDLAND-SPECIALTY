Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: June 22, 2026

Your privacy matters

This policy explains what information Midland Specialty may collect through this website and how that information may be used.

1. Introduction

Midland Specialty Inc. (“Midland Specialty,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information collected through this website, including information submitted through quote requests, contact forms, and employment applications.

By using this website or submitting information through it, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Information you provide

We may collect information you choose to submit, including:

  • Name, company, email address, telephone number, and location.
  • Information about your logistics, transportation, warehousing, security advisory, or supply-chain needs.
  • Messages and other information included in quote or contact requests.
  • Employment application information, including work experience, LinkedIn profile, résumé or CV, and other materials you provide.

Technical information

Our website or hosting providers may automatically receive limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, pages viewed, and timestamps. This information may be used to operate, secure, and improve the website.

3. How We May Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to questions, quote requests, and service inquiries.
  • Evaluate potential client relationships and provide requested information.
  • Review employment applications and communicate with candidates.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, users, and business.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, communications, document storage, recruiting, analytics, security, or business operations, subject to appropriate contractual or legal obligations.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or governmental requests; investigate fraud or security concerns; protect rights or safety; or support a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of business assets.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for site functionality, security, performance, or user preferences. Browser settings may allow you to block or delete cookies, although some features may not work correctly afterward.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to inquiries, evaluating applications, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary according to the information and applicable requirements.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion. To make a request, email midlandspecialtyinc@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information when permitted or required by law.

9. Employment Applications

Candidate information is used to evaluate qualifications, communicate about opportunities, maintain recruiting records, and meet legal obligations. Submitting an application does not create an employment relationship or guarantee an interview or offer.

10. Third-Party Links and Services

This website may link to websites or services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Midland Specialty is not responsible for those practices.

11. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for business users and employment candidates and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

12. International Visitors

If you access this website from outside the United States, information you submit may be processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate. Applicable privacy rights may vary by location.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The “last updated” date above identifies the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
